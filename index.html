Workflow reference

Content Planning & Approval Use Case

This diagram explains how client requirements are translated into a proposed monthly content plan, reviewed, exported, approved, and assigned for production.

1

Select Brand

Choose the client and month, then review the current planning requirements.

↓
2

Review Brand Bible + SOP

Check non-negotiables, visual rules, tone, priorities, important business information, latest assets and operational SOPs.

↓
3

Create Strategy Brief

Define the content goal, product/service focus, hook, audience reason, content direction, caption, CTA, required imagery, references and avoidances.

↓
4

Create Content

Produce one clear piece of content that follows the brief and brand rules.

↓
5

Creator Check

Complete the editable Internal QC checklist. Reels automatically include Premium Music Vibes QC.

↓
6

Internal Review

A second internal quality check confirms the work is accurate, current, on-brand and purposeful.

↓
7

Nicole / Lead Approval

Nicole confirms the work meets Studio 5's creative and quality standard.

↓
8

Ready for Client

This quality gate unlocks only when all required QC items, Creator Check, Internal Review and Nicole / Lead Approval are complete.

↓
9

Client Review

Content is approved or returned with a tracked revision/rejection reason for continuous quality improvement.

System purpose: The workflow keeps strategy, scheduling, internal review, client approval, export, and production handoff connected in one source of truth.
