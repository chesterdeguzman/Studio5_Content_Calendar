Production + QA workflow

Content Production & Quality Gate

The original planning workflow now includes mandatory strategy, internal QC and Nicole's lead approval before anything is ready for the client.

1

Client Brief + Brand Bible

Review the selected client's current rules, UK English requirements, brand details, imagery rules, product/service priorities, facts and do-not-use instructions.

↓
2

Mandatory Strategy Brief

Define the content goal, exact product/service focus, hook, audience benefit, content direction, caption, CTA, required assets, references and anything to avoid.

↓
3

Create the Content

Produce one focused post or reel using the latest relevant imagery and the approved brand direction. Reels must have one clear purpose rather than becoming a generic montage.

↓
4

Creator Check

The creator completes the editable QC checklist, checking brief accuracy, brand consistency, UK English, factual details, imagery, hook, caption and CTA. Reels include Premium Music Vibe QC.

↓
5

Internal Review

A second reviewer checks the work against the strategy brief and Brand Bible. Any failed review records a structured reason so recurring quality issues can be measured.

↓
6

Nicole / Lead Approval

Nicole provides the final internal creative approval. This makes the lead standard an explicit system gate rather than an informal expectation.

↓
7

Proceed to Create

Each QC stage is completed in its own review window. Once Creator Check, Internal Review and Nicole / Lead Approval are approved, Nicole can mark the item Proceed to Create. The Calendar status is then automatically set to Approved.

↓
8

Client Review → Production

The client can approve or request revisions. Approved work proceeds to production/publishing; revisions return to the team with a clear reason and feedback.

Quality principle: What are we selling or communicating? Is it immediately clear? Does it follow the brief exactly? Is the imagery current? Is the hook strong? Would we stop and engage with it ourselves?
